Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magnito Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you play GO! Blocks (the “Game”) on Android or iOS. By using the Game you acknowledge this policy. Please also read our Terms of Use.

In short

  • The Game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Built-in analytics and attribution services collect device information, device identifiers and gameplay events so we can improve the Game and measure the campaigns that promote it.
  • The Game currently shows no ads. We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can limit tracking in your device settings, and you can ask us to access or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Magnito Studio, based in the Philippines, develops the Game and is responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and technical data such as frame rate and loading times.
  • Identifiers: your device’s advertising identifier (the Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt), the identifier for vendors (IDFV) on iOS, and random installation identifiers created by the services listed in section 6.
  • Network information: IP address, from which an approximate location such as your country or city may be derived, and connection type.
  • Gameplay events: for example when the Game starts, when a level is started, continued, completed, failed or left, the level number, your progress in a level, time played and session length.
  • Install and campaign information: how you found the Game, for example which advertisement or store page led to your install.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.

Your game progress and settings (sound, music and vibration) are stored only on your device. Using Reset Progress in the Game’s settings deletes them, and so does uninstalling the Game.

3. How the information is collected

Apart from emails you send us, the information above is collected automatically by software development kits (“SDKs”) built into the Game, listed in section 6, while you use the Game. The Game does not use web cookies; the SDKs rely on the device identifiers described above.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the Game and keep it working, for example to find bugs and performance problems. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a working Game.
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve it, for example to balance level difficulty. Legal basis: your consent and our legitimate interests.
  • To measure the advertising campaigns that promote the Game on other apps and websites (install attribution). Legal basis: your consent.
  • To comply with the law and respond to your requests. Legal basis: legal obligations.

Where we rely on consent, you give it through the privacy notice shown when the Game first starts and, on iOS, through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 8.

The Game currently does not display advertisements. If we add ads, we will update this policy first.

5. How we share information

We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed in section 6, which process it for the purposes above;
  • with our publishing partner, which helps us market the Game and analyze how it performs;
  • when required by law or to protect our rights, our users or the public; and
  • with a buyer or successor if the Game or our business is sold or transferred.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

  • AppsFlyerInstall attribution and measurement of marketing campaigns. AppsFlyer privacy policy
  • AppMetrica (Yandex)App analytics. Yandex privacy policy
  • Facebook SDK (Meta)App events used to measure advertising campaigns run on Meta platforms. Meta privacy policy
  • GameAnalyticsGame analytics. GameAnalytics privacy notice

Advertising identifiers are provided by your device’s operating system: Google on Android and Apple on iOS.

7. International transfers

These services may process information on servers outside your country, including in the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States and other countries. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your choices and rights

Limit tracking

  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • iOS: choose “Ask App Not to Track” in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or turn tracking off for GO! Blocks in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • To stop all collection, uninstall the Game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your information, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To make a request, email magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com. Because the Game has no accounts, please include your device’s advertising ID (if available) so we can find the data concerned. We will reply within 30 days, or within the time your local law requires.

  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to opt out of the “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, which may include the campaign measurement described above. Use the tracking controls above or email us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • Philippines: under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the rights to be informed, access, object, erasure, rectification, data portability and to damages, and you may complain to the National Privacy Commission.

9. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes in section 4. The services in section 6 keep data according to their own retention periods. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Data on your device stays until you reset your progress or uninstall the Game.

10. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from children under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We and our service providers use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the Game or on its store page.
