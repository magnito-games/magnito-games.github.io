Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magnito Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you play GO! Blocks (the “Game”) on Android or iOS. Please also read our Terms of Use.

In short

  • The Game does not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Magnito Studio, based in the Philippines, makes the Game. You can reach us at magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The Game contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking software, and it does not use your device’s advertising identifier. It does not send your gameplay, device information or any identifier to us or to anyone else.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins and settings (sound, music and vibration) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Using Reset Progress in the Game’s settings deletes them, and so does uninstalling the Game.

4. Links and app stores

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Rate Us buttons open this website or your app store outside the Game. Google Play and the App Store handle your download and any crash reports you choose to share with developers under their own privacy policies: Google and Apple.

5. Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

6. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal information from the Game, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that email at any time. Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or the Philippines), you may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game starts collecting any information, for example to show ads, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact us

Magnito Studio · Philippines · magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com
