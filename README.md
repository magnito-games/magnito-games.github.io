# Symsey Cruz — Portfolio

Personal portfolio site for **Symsey Cruz**, an enthusiast mobile developer (iOS & Android) — games *and* apps.

**Games** — *Web Master* and *Layer Man 3D*, developed at Kobgames Studio and published by Azur Games (60M+ downloads combined); plus *Squeezee* and *GO! Blocks*, released under his own studio **Magnito Studio**.

**Apps** — *Bandee* (turns a song into a chart your band can rehearse from) and *Pikolbol* (pickleball scorer with an Apple Watch companion), both under **Magnito Studio**.

Live at https://magnito-games.github.io
