Squeezee · Pikolbol

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magnito Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use our apps Squeezee and Pikolbol (each an “App”) on iOS, watchOS or Android. By using an App you acknowledge this policy. Our other apps have their own policies: GO! Blocks and Bandee.

In short

  • Squeezee has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. It shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, which use device information and advertising identifiers.
  • Pikolbol works without an account. If you sign in, we store the profile and play information you create so you can play with others, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.
  • Pikolbol uses Firebase for crash reports, app analytics and notifications. It uses your location and camera only for the features that need them, and only if you allow it.
  • We do not sell your personal information. You can limit tracking in your device settings and ask us to access or delete your data.

1. Who we are

Magnito Studio, based in the Philippines, develops the Apps and is responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com.

2. Squeezee

Squeezee never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

Squeezee shows interstitial, banner and optional rewarded ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which uses Unity Ads to serve them. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, these services automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language and region settings, and app version.
  • Identifiers: the Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS, the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; and identifiers created by the ad services.
  • Network information: IP address, from which an approximate location such as your country or city may be derived.
  • Ad interactions: which ads are shown, whether you watch, skip or tap them, and whether you finish a rewarded ad.

Where the law requires it, Squeezee asks for your consent before your data is used for personalized ads and passes your choice to LevelPlay. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

3. Pikolbol

Without an account

You can keep score without creating an account. Your matches, scores and settings are stored on your device. On Apple Watch, the iPhone and Watch apps sync directly with each other. When devices near each other share a match, they connect directly over your local network or Bluetooth, not through our servers.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. When you sign in, we process:

  • Account information: an account identifier and the email address provided by Apple or Google. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address.
  • Profile information you add: your display name, an optional profile photo, your self-rated level, your region and any rating you choose to enter.
  • Play and social information: sessions you host or join, match rosters and results, scorekeeper roles, streaks, clubs, and the players you follow.

Location

If you allow it, Pikolbol uses your location while the app is open to show Open Play sessions near you. When you host a session, its venue location is saved with the session and shown on the map to nearby players. Pikolbol requests location only at venue level (about 100 meters) and does not track your location in the background.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan a match QR code so you can join a game. Camera images are not stored or sent to us. If you pick a profile photo, Pikolbol receives only the photo you choose.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token is registered with Firebase Cloud Messaging so Pikolbol can notify you about your games and sessions.

Analytics and crash reports

Pikolbol uses Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used, for example screens viewed and actions taken, together with an app instance identifier and device information. It uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which include crash logs, device model and operating system version.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Pikolbol account at any time from the app’s Settings. This deletes your account and the profile information linked to it from our servers. Information stored only on your device stays there until you delete the app.

4. Why we use information

  • To provide the Apps and their features, such as scoring, sign-in, sessions, sync and notifications. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in running the Apps.
  • To show, limit and measure ads in Squeezee and prevent ad fraud. Legal basis: your consent where required, and otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free game.
  • To keep the Apps working and improve them, using crash reports and analytics. Legal basis: our legitimate interests and, where required, your consent.
  • To comply with the law and respond to your requests. Legal basis: legal obligations.

5. How we share information

We share information only:

  • with other players: in Pikolbol, your display name, profile photo, level, and the sessions you host, including their venue location, are visible to other players;
  • with the service providers listed in section 6, which process it for the purposes above;
  • when required by law or to protect our rights, our users or the public; and
  • with a buyer or successor if an App or our business is sold or transferred.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

8. Your choices and rights

Limit tracking and permissions

  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • iOS: choose “Ask App Not to Track” in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or turn tracking off for Squeezee in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Turn location, camera, photos or notifications on or off for Pikolbol at any time in your device settings.
  • Delete your Pikolbol account in the app’s Settings. To stop all collection, uninstall the App.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your information, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To make a request, email magnitogamesstudio@gmail.com. For Squeezee, which has no accounts, please include your device’s advertising ID if available. For Pikolbol, please write from, or include, the email address linked to your account. We will reply within 30 days, or within the time your local law requires.

  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to opt out of the “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, which includes personalized ads in Squeezee. Use the tracking controls above or email us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • Philippines: under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the rights to be informed, access, object, erasure, rectification, data portability and to damages, and you may complain to the National Privacy Commission.

9. How long we keep information

We keep Pikolbol account information for as long as your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. The services in section 6 keep data according to their own retention periods. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Data on your device stays until you delete the App.

If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.
